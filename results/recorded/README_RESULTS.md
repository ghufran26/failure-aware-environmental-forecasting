# Failure-aware environmental forecasting audit (ESD v4)

Target journal: Environment Systems and Decisions (Springer Nature).

All cohorts are reported as retrospective or independent external replication. The software never upgrades a user declaration into confirmatory evidence. All twelve Beijing stations are evaluated, including leave-one-station-out transfer.

All fitted preprocessing uses development data. Six research questions are evaluated, each with three vector PDF figures and one publication table. External confirmation adds three figures and one table. Numeric plot sources, CSV/LaTeX tables, captions, source, configuration, checksums, and resumable checkpoints are included.

Detector feasibility requires both observed-policy FAR and the 95th percentile of dependent-null FAR to meet the target. Guarded alarm adaptation validates every candidate refit on already observed pre-update labels. Failed or disabled detectors remain visible. Coverage is empirical rather than an unconditional exchangeability guarantee.

RQ5 reports an environmental monitoring decision sensitivity analysis using frozen policy thresholds, false-negative/false-positive cost ratios, and an explicit abstention cost. These costs are transparent scenarios, not claimed stakeholder utilities.

Energy telemetry may be unavailable or estimated; no carbon claim is generated. Passing software QA establishes reproducibility and technical completeness, not journal acceptance or universal environmental validity.

Checkpoints are matched to source, scientific configuration, package versions, inputs and task parameters. The final dissemination ZIP contains exactly the payloads listed in its checksum manifest; resumable checkpoints remain in partial phase archives.
